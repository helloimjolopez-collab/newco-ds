The one honest constraint: in Light, Elevated/Base is already #fefcfc, a hair off pure white, so you cannot stack three ever-lighter fills — there is no room below the ceiling. Depth in Light is read from the elevation shadow (you already ship --elevation-sheet/widget/lift/overlay), which is why Elevated/Raised is #ffffff + a lift shadow. In Midnight there are no real shadows, so the same rung is a visibly lighter grey. That is the correct split, and it is why the old darker “medium/Contrast” never read as elevation.
